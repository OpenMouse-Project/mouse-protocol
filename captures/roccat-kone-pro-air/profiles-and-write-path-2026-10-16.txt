# Per-profile read pages and the write path (2026-10-16)

Second session on the same unit, this time with ROCCAT Swarm installed and
driving the mouse, observed from a passive node-hid listener on interface 2
while the Swarm UI was operated. It settles what the earlier captures could
not: where the settings block lives in the reply stream, and what our
user-mode writes actually do to the mouse.

## The five `90 00 26` reply pages are the five onboard profiles

Swarm exposes five profile slots ("配置文件组1"…"配置文件组5"). When the
device page attaches, the mouse streams five `90 00 26` replies, one per
profile. Fields, aligned on the page's own header `90 00 26 00 00 04 20 00
92 2c`:

```text
b10    profile index (01..05)
b11    that profile's polling code (00=1000 Hz .. 03=125 Hz, same dict as the write)
b12    00
b13    00 empty / 01 valid / 02 the profile the UI is editing
b14-23 five DPI stages, u16 little-endian, 50/unit (e.g. 08 00 = 400 DPI)
b24    lift-off distance code (06 = Low)
b25    00
b26    80 / 06 / 00 (profile-dependent; unresolved)
b27    LED timeout minutes (0f = 15)
```

Captured order (1000 Hz era, mouse awake, profiles 2..5 configured, profile 1
blank):

```text
90 00 26 00 00 04 20 00 92 2c 02 00 00 02 08 00 10 00 15 00 20 00 40 00 06 00 06 0f ...
90 00 26 00 00 04 20 00 92 2c 03 00 00 01 08 00 10 00 18 00 20 00 40 00 06 00 00 0f ...
90 00 26 00 00 04 20 00 92 2c 04 00 00 01 08 00 10 00 18 00 20 00 40 00 06 00 00 05 ...
90 00 26 00 00 04 20 00 92 2c 05 00 00 01 08 00 10 00 18 00 20 00 40 00 06 00 00 05 ...
90 00 26 00 00 04 20 00 92 2c 01 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 06 00 00 0f ...
```

The profile the UI was editing (index 2) carries the stages **400 / 800 /
1050 / 1600 / 3200** — byte-for-byte the values the UI's own "DPI 切换开关"
card displays (`400`, `800`, `1050`, `1600`, `3200`). The 1050 is the unit
owner's own value, not a side effect of any probe.

## UI edits land in these pages (one-session differential)

With the listener running, the UI was used to switch the edited profile's
polling rate 1000 → 125 Hz. Restarting Swarm re-triggered the stream, and the
same profile's page changed exactly one byte:

```text
before:  90 00 26 00 00 04 20 00 92 2c 02 00 00 02 08 00 10 00 15 00 20 00 40 00 ...
after:   90 00 26 00 00 04 20 00 92 2c 02 03 00 02 08 00 10 00 15 00 20 00 40 00 ...
```

`b11` 00 → 03 (125 Hz). That identifies the polling field and proves the
vendor's own write path reaches the mouse.

## Read page vs. write frame: a +5 byte shift

Lining the page up with the SignalRGB plugin's write frame (`Plugins/Roccat/
Roccat_Kone_Pro_Air_Mouse.js`) puts every established field at write offset
+ 5:

```text
write frame            10 50 14 00 01 <poll@6> <angle@7> 00 <stages u16 @9..18> <lod@19> 00 80 <led@22> 00 <deb@24>
read page  90 00 26 00 00 04 20 00 92 2c <prof@10> <poll@11> 00 <state@13> <stages u16 @14..23> <lod@24> 00 <b26@26> <led@27> ...
```

So the stored settings block is the plugin's layout, five u16 stage slots
(not two interleaved byte lists), and the page is that block shifted right by
five bytes behind a ten-byte read header.

## What our own writes did

- Frames sent by hand on interface 2 — the plugin layout with stage 1 set to
  500 (u16 `0a 00`) and, separately, the two-interleaved-lists shape — were
  **accepted and echoed** (`50 14 03 00 ec fa`), but a fresh stream after each
  showed **no profile's stage 1 at 500**. The stored tables were unchanged.
- On this unit the vendor writes are not visible on any HID interface at all:
  a listener on interfaces 0/1/2/3 during UI edits captured the UI's read
  polling loops (`10 71 03`, `10 72 03`, `10 11 03`, `10 50 03`) but no
  settings write on any of them.
- The vendor's device DLL (`data\2C8E\2C8E.dll`) is a hidapi fork whose
  `hid_write` is called in exactly four places, all in the calibration
  module; there is no settings-write code in it.

Read together: on this unit a settings write from a plain user-mode HID
handle is acknowledged but not applied, and the vendor applies settings
through a channel a user-mode capture cannot see. The driver's write frames
are therefore best described as **unverified on hardware**, not verified.

## Backing files

Raw listener logs from this session (not shipped here): the awake stream, the
125 Hz differential, and the status probes. The status probe while the mouse
was asleep answers `90 0a 03 00 ec fa 0d 00` with an all-zero body, versus
`90 0a 04 00 00 64 …` when awake — the same empty/no-data dichotomy the
settings echo shows, and why the Swarm device page sits on its loading
spinner and shows placeholder 0 % battery until the mouse answers.
